# Deckl Polish

Finish the details that make a coherent design feel deliberate. Preserve the chosen direction. Do not substitute a new aesthetic during the final pass.

## Inspect the whole surface

Read project instructions and inspect the affected routes and shared components. Review top, middle, bottom, navigation, and secondary states when browser tools are available. Check project conventions and available validation commands.

List concrete inconsistencies before editing. Prioritize issues that affect reading, interaction, or the impression of finish. For review-only requests, deliver this list without edits.

## Preserve approved character

Keep before views when possible. Identify the approved type, image treatment, expressive layout, and motion details that give the page its identity. Finish these rather than normalizing them into a different aesthetic. If a cleanup makes the composition less expressive or less balanced, correct your own change. Judge before and after at the same viewport and scroll position.

## Correct the details

- Optical alignment: align icon/text pairs, button labels, baselines, and image edges; check the actual rendered result rather than numeric equality alone.
- Type finish: resolve awkward wrapping, clipped glyphs, inconsistent weights, and unreadable secondary text without rewriting factual meaning.
- Spacing: remove accidental gaps and inconsistent group spacing; preserve intentional negative space.
- Shape and surfaces: reconcile borders, radii, shadows, and separators with the established system.
- Color: ensure actions, metadata, focus, and status use their intended roles and remain readable.
- Media: fix broken assets, distorted crops, low-resolution presentation, and missing reserved dimensions.
- Interaction: inspect hover, focus, pressed, disabled, loading, empty, error, and success states where applicable to affected components.
- Completeness: find dead destinations, leftover prototype copy, debug UI, inconsistent labels, and missing relevant page metadata.

Do not invent missing legal text, product claims, testimonials, or destinations to make the site appear complete. Report them as specific outstanding needs. Preserve tracking, form semantics, and existing state handling.

Make bounded fixes. If the real problem is a weak concept or fundamentally broken information structure, explain it separately rather than undertaking a silent redesign. Do not reduce every radius or replace the font merely because a different aesthetic is possible.

## Validate and close

Revisit the changed areas at narrow and wide sizes. Exercise affected controls with keyboard and pointer where available. Run relevant project checks and inspect console or asset failures when browser tools permit.

Report completed fixes, actual verification, and any release blockers with their user impact. A build passing does not establish visual quality, accessibility conformance, or a working backend.

Stop when observable finish defects within scope are resolved. Do not repeatedly change acceptable details for novelty, add a new animation system, or redesign unrelated pages.
